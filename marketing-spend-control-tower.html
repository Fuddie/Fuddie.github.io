Marketing Spend Control Tower

I built a tested analytics workflow that connects campaign spend, budgets, attributed revenue and refunds. It shows current performance, estimates month-end spend and prioritises campaigns that are likely to exceed budget.

Independent portfolio project using reproducible synthetic data. The dashboard and Wren queries run locally, while the repository contains the complete models, tests, orchestration and setup instructions.

01 / BUSINESS PROBLEM

Marketing teams need an early warning, not a month-end surprise.

Campaign reporting often separates platform spend, budgets, orders and refunds. That makes it difficult to answer whether spend is on plan, whether attributed revenue is reliable and which campaigns need intervention.

I designed one workflow around five practical questions:

  • How much has been spent and what net revenue was attributed to it?
  • Which campaigns are ahead of their expected spending pace?
  • Which campaigns are projected to exceed budget?
  • Which alerts are most urgent?
  • Can the dashboard, alert service and semantic queries use the same definitions?

02 / ARCHITECTURE

A layered workflow from source extracts to decisions.

Each layer has a clear responsibility, with quality checks before reporting models are used by the dashboard or alert service.

  1. 01 / INGEST

    Python + DuckDB

    Six partitioned CSV extracts are loaded transactionally into typed Bronze tables.

  2. 02 / VALIDATE

    Bronze checks

    Seventeen checks cover relationships, dates, amounts, currencies and attribution rules.

  3. 03 / MODEL

    dbt

    Staging, intermediate and gold models apply attribution, budget history and pacing logic.

  4. 04 / USE

    Airflow + reporting

    Airflow runs the workflow. Streamlit, alerts and Wren consume the gold layer.

03 / GOLD MODELS

Three marts serve distinct decisions.

Daily performance

One row per campaign and date with spend, attributed conversions, gross revenue, completed refunds and attributed net revenue.

fct_campaign_daily_performance

Daily pacing

Adds month-to-date spend, expected spend, projected month-end spend, ROAS, pacing ratio and projected budget breach status.

mart_campaign_daily_pacing

Campaign alerts

Returns current warning and critical conditions in business review order, including the reason and supporting financial values.

04 / METRIC AND ALERT RULES

Definitions are explicit and shared.

Core business rules
RuleDefinition
Attributed net revenueCompleted attributed order revenue minus completed attributed refunds.
ROASAttributed net revenue divided by spend.
Expected spendMonthly budget allocated across active campaign days and accumulated through the reporting date.
Pacing ratioMonth-to-date spend divided by expected spend to date.
Critical alertPost-end spend, spend without a budget, or month-to-date spend above budget.
Warning alertPacing ratio above 1.10.

06 / RELIABILITY

Quality controls are part of the workflow.

Source and Bronze controls

Complete extracts are required. Ingestion runs inside a transaction, rejects older snapshots and closes database connections after use.

dbt validation

The final build completed with 3 table models, 8 view models and 59 data tests. All 70 results passed with no warnings or errors.

Business assertions

Custom tests protect the campaign-date grain, net revenue reconciliation and the rule that post-end spend must be critical.

Safe reporting

The dashboard opens DuckDB in read-only mode, caches results for five minutes and keeps email delivery disabled unless credentials and the send flag are supplied.

07 / COMPATIBILITY DECISION

Handle the DuckDB version boundary explicitly.

The analytics environment uses DuckDB 2.0.0, while Wren 0.15.0 uses DuckDB 1.5.5. Connecting Wren directly to the newer database produced an incompatible API path.

I added a reproducible snapshot process. The main environment exports the three gold marts to Parquet, then the Wren environment rebuilds its own compatible DuckDB file transactionally. Generated snapshots and local connection files remain outside Git.

This keeps the analytics warehouse current without hiding the version constraint or requiring a manual table copy.

08 / TRADE-OFFS

Keep the project honest about its scope.

Synthetic data

The project demonstrates modelling and operational decisions without using private campaign or customer data. Production use would replace the CSV generator with platform and commerce connectors.

Local warehouse

DuckDB is appropriate for a reproducible portfolio workflow. A multi-user deployment would move the marts to a managed warehouse and add role-based access.

Local dashboard

The Streamlit application is documented and reproducible but is not presented as a permanently hosted production service.

Alert delivery

Alerts run in preview mode by default. Resend delivery requires explicit environment variables and a send flag.
