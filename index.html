01 / ABOUT

Analytics engineering between the warehouse and the business.

I build the layer that makes analytics dependable: clean source-aligned models, reusable transformation logic, tested fact and dimension tables, and consistent business metrics for reporting and decision-making.

My background spans BI engineering, data quality, customer operations and analytics. That combination helps me translate business questions into data models that are understandable to both technical and non-technical teams.

I also enjoy improving data literacy. I have led workshops covering Looker, BigQuery, modelling concepts and effective data exploration, and I have mentored junior team members on analytics engineering workflows.
