01 / ABOUT

Building the data layer between source systems and reporting.

I work on data transformation, modelling, metric definition and reporting layers. My focus is making sure analysts and business teams have consistent data they can use without rebuilding the same logic in different places.

My background includes BI engineering, data quality, customer operations and analytics. That experience helps me translate business questions into data models and reports that are practical to use and maintain.
